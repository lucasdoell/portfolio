## Design Context

### Users

Primary audience: **design-conscious engineers** who land on the portfolio from links, social, GitHub, or word of mouth. Not a job-hunt funnel — Lucas is not actively hiring for himself and isn't optimizing for recruiter scan patterns.

Visitor behavior: arrives curious, spends 30–90 seconds scanning, leaves with an impression of *whether Lucas has taste*. The site is a personal artifact, redesigned periodically because the craft is the point.

The thesis being communicated: in an era where code is cheap to generate, **taste is the differentiator**. The site is the evidence — its existence and quality matter more than any copy inside it.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **minimalist, inviting, confident.**

- **Minimalist** — restraint over density. Negative space is load-bearing. Standard portfolio furniture (card grids, icon tiles, feature blocks) is explicitly rejected.
- **Inviting** — warm, not sterile. Off-white and cream tones instead of clinical white. Warm charcoal instead of pure black. The reader should feel welcomed into a space, not inspected inside a lab.
- **Confident** — assertive typography and asymmetric composition. No over-explaining, no hedging, no "hi recruiter, here's my stack" SaaS-template voice.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Reference touchpoints (provided by Lucas):**

- **Hervé Baillargeon** — corner-anchored massive letterforms spelling a name across the viewport, tiny mono meta-labels (`HB`, `+ INFO`, `DIRECTOR`), cream ground, asymmetric small imagery.
- **Olga Prudka** — distinctive serif-with-italic display, sage-neutral ground, a single small photograph floating off-center, elegant editorial feel.
- **Bamboo Crowd** — oversized grotesk sans filling the viewport, one bold accent (red circle), Swiss-modernist clarity.
- **Constance Souville** — editorial serif display + tiny metadata captions (`MTL 16:20:42`, availability date, "EMAIL"), warm stone ground, small colored button-shapes as punctuation.

**Common DNA across all four:**

- Off-white / warm-neutral grounds (cream, stone, bone, sage) — never pure `#fff`
- Display typography at extreme scale, often viewport-spanning
- Small metadata labels carrying supporting detail (dates, locations, status, captions)
- Asymmetric editorial composition with generous negative space
- Minimal palette; at most one small strategic accent
- Gallery / magazine feel, not SaaS-product feel

**Theme:** Both light and dark, with view transitions planned as a later enhancement.

- Light mode: warm off-white ground (cream/bone, around `oklch(0.96 0.008 85)`), near-black ink with a touch of warmth.
- Dark mode: warm charcoal (not pure black, around `oklch(0.15 0.005 60)`), cream text.

**Anti-references (what this must not look like):**

- **The current site** — pure gray monochrome, system font stack, safe templated sections. Functional but forgettable.
- **Generic dev portfolio tropes** — icon-grid project cards, monospace-as-technical-signal, terminal aesthetics, cyan-on-dark heroes, bento-box feature grids, icon-above-heading patterns.
- **Rounded cards with drop shadows** — the default AI output.
- **"Modern SaaS landing page"** — DM Sans / Inter / Outfit + purple-to-blue gradient + rounded pill buttons.

**Color palette direction:**

- Dominant: warm off-white / cream in light; warm charcoal in dark. Ink is warm near-black; light-mode text on dark is warm cream.
- Accent: one color, used in less than 5% of visual weight. Not yet chosen — candidates suggested by references include muted vermillion, aged brass, oxblood, ochre. Avoid neon, cyan, electric blue, and any purple-to-blue gradient.
- Lucas has specifically said off-whites are welcome; no hard color bans beyond the AI-tell palette.

**Typography direction (to be chosen during implementation):**

- Reject all fonts in the impeccable skill's reflex list: Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Fraunces, Newsreader, Instrument Serif/Sans, IBM Plex family, Syne, Cormorant, Playfair Display, Crimson family, Lora.
- Aim for pairing: one distinctive display face + one refined body face.
- The references favor two directions — pick one per project iteration, don't mix:
  - **Swiss grotesk direction:** something in the Söhne / Neue Haas Grotesk / ABC Diatype / Graphik / ABC Ginto family.
  - **Editorial serif direction:** something in the GT Super / Migra / PP Editorial New / Reckless / PP Right Serif family.
- Monospace is allowed **only** for small meta-labels (timestamps, status, section markers). Never for body text and never as a "technical vibe" signal.

### Design Principles

1. **Type is the hero.** Every view should have one dominant typographic moment at extreme scale — imagery and metadata orbit around it. If a layout can collapse to "big type + tiny labels + one photo," that's probably the right layout.
2. **Warm over cold.** Default to off-white / cream / warm charcoal. Pure `#fff` and `#000` are banned. Every neutral carries a hint of warmth in its hue.
3. **Negative space is content.** A page that is 60% empty with one confident anchor reads as more designed than a page tiled with UI. Resist the urge to fill.
4. **Meta-labels do the work cards used to do.** Dates, locations, section labels, status, captions — set small (12–13px), often caps or mono, placed asymmetrically. Use them to carry detail that would otherwise force a card.
5. **One accent, used rarely.** The palette is two neutrals plus one accent. The accent appears in at most one small element per view and earns its place.
6. **If it's the default, it's wrong.** No card grids. No icon-above-heading. No hero-with-gradient. No monospace-as-developer-signal. No pill buttons with drop shadows. When a convention feels automatic, that's the signal to do something else.
